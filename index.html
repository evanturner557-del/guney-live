A village getting quieter

In 1985, 3,678 people lived in Güney. In 2022, 1,033 did. When people leave, houses close and memories leave with them.

Güney was a town with its own municipality from 1968 until 2013, when the population fell below 2,000.

This page is a small door back in, for anyone who is from here or would like to be.

From the archive

Records from 1919 list the village as Günü. Some of what it was is still here. Some survives only in photographs.
